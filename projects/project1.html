Project Overview

The project focuses on developing a smart robotic vehicle capable of performing surveillance tasks in environments where direct human involvement may be risky.

The vehicle combines an embedded controller, motor driver, sensors, IoT connectivity and a camera-based monitoring system to enable remote operation and real-time observation.

CORE IDEA

Remote surveillance without putting humans at risk.

02

The Problem

Surveillance in border regions and hazardous environments can expose personnel to dangerous conditions. Continuous monitoring and physical inspection may also require significant human effort.

03

Our Solution

A remotely controlled intelligent surveillance vehicle.

The robotic vehicle can be controlled remotely while collecting information from its surrounding environment. IoT connectivity enables communication between the vehicle and the remote monitoring interface.
