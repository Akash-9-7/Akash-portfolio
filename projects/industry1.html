During my 2nd year of Engineering, I had the opportunity to visit the ISRO Propulsion Complex, Mahendragiri. The visit provided valuable exposure to India's space technology environment and the importance of propulsion systems in space missions.

It was an exciting experience to understand the scale of engineering involved in rocket propulsion, testing and large-scale technical operations.
