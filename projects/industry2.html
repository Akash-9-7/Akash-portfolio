EXPERIENCE

Understanding

In my 3rd year of Engineering, I had the opportunity to visit the Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant as part of an industrial visit. The visit provided valuable exposure to one of India's major nuclear power generation facilities.

It was a unique opportunity to observe the scale of industrial infrastructure and understand how engineering, safety and systematic operations come together in a large-scale power generation environment.
